# Gemini prompts: the 7 dance-move figures

How to use:
1. Open gemini.google.com in your own browser and start ONE new chat for all 7 images (keeps the character consistent).
2. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** first, then add one **MOVE** line. Ask Gemini to generate the image.
3. If the dancer drifts from the previous image, say: "Same character, same outfit, same style as before. Only change the pose."
4. Save each result as `move1.png` ... `move7.png` and upload them to the Claude session. The poster layout picks up `figures/move1.png` ... `figures/move7.png` automatically (numbers, arrows, captions and section titles are added by the layout, so do NOT ask Gemini for text).

## STYLE BLOCK (paste first, every time)

```
Flat vector illustration, full body, front view, one young woman dancing.
Style: stylised mid-century poster art made of angular faceted shapes, three-tone flat shading, no outlines, no gradients, no texture.
Character: warm tan skin, long straight dark navy-black hair with a side part, sage-green halter crop top, high-waisted wide-leg navy jeans, white sneakers with a sage stripe. Friendly, relaxed, confident dance posture with the weight on one leg.
Palette only: sage green #7f9669, deep navy #23455e, tan skin tones, white.
Background: solid pure white (#FFFFFF), nothing else. No floor, no shadow, no text, no numbers, no arrows, no props.
The entire body must fit inside the frame with a small white margin all round. Show both arms and both hands clearly with five fingers each.
```

## MOVE lines (add ONE per image)

1. `Pose: both hands resting on the sides of her head, elbows pointing out to the sides.`
2. `Pose: both hands resting on her shoulders, elbows pointing down and slightly out.`
3. `Pose: both hands on her hips, elbows pointing out to the sides.`
4. `Pose: both hands tapping the center of her chest, elbows slightly out.`
5. `Pose: her RIGHT arm (on the viewer's left) stretched straight out to the side at shoulder height; her LEFT hand reaching across her body to touch her right forearm near the elbow.`
6. `Pose: her RIGHT arm (on the viewer's left) stretched straight out to the side at shoulder height; her LEFT hand pressed flat on her heart (her chest, on the viewer's right side).`
7. `Pose: both hands clapped together in the center in front of her chest, elbows out to the sides.`

Tips
- Ask for a square image. If the figure is cut off, add: "zoom out so the whole body fits."
- If the background is not pure white, add: "Pure white background only."
- Right/left check: her RIGHT is on the viewer's LEFT.
